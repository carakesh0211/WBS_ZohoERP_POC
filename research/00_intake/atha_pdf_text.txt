1. Executive Summary

The objective is to establish project-wise Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) and Capital Work in Progress (CWIP) 

control within the Zoho ERP environment. The proposed design ensures that approved budgets are monitored 

not only against vendor bills (actual cost), but also against Purchase Orders (committed cost), thereby providing 

management with a true view of available CAPEX budget before further procurement is approved.

Core Control Principle:  Available Budget = Current Approved Budget - Actual CWIP - Open PO

 

Commitments

2. Business Objectives

• 

Create a unique CAPEX / CWIP Project Code for every approved capital project.

• 

Maintain project-level and budget-head-level approved budgets.

• 

Validate budget availability before Purchase Order approval.

• 

Treat approved/open Purchase Orders as committed expenditure.

• 

Convert commitments into actual CWIP as vendor bills are booked without double counting.

• 

Provide controlled budget revision and exceptional approval for overruns.

• 

Maintain an audit trail from budget approval through procurement, accounting and final capitalization.

• 

Provide management dashboards for Budget vs Commitment vs Actual vs Available Budget.

7. Purchase Order Budget Validation Logic

At PR/PO approval, the system should read the CAPEX Code and Budget Head and calculate the remaining 

budget. Open PO commitments must be considered even where the corresponding vendor bill has not yet been 

received.

Example: ₹80L Budget - ₹35L Actual CWIP - ₹30L Open PO Commitment = ₹15L Available

If a new PO of ₹20L is proposed, the transaction exceeds available budget by ₹5L. The system should route the 

transaction to an exceptional approval / budget revision process rather than treating it as a normal within-

budget PO.

13. Key Controls & Audit Trail

• 

Mandatory CAPEX Code and Budget Head on applicable capital procurement.

• 

No silent overwrite of original approved budget.

• 

Every revision to retain requestor, approver, date, reason and approval reference.

• 

PO amendments to trigger revalidation for incremental value.

• 

PO cancellation/closure to release unused commitment.

• 

Bill posting to reduce open commitment and increase actual CWIP.

• 

Closed/capitalized projects to prevent further procurement unless reopened through approval.

• 

Dashboard drill-down from project to PR, PO, receipt/GRN and vendor bill.

15. Expected Outcome for Atha Group

The proposed solution will provide Atha Group with a controlled and auditable CAPEX lifecycle in which 

management can see not only what has already been spent, but also what has been committed through Purchase

 

Orders. This enables proactive prevention of budget overruns, improved project-wise CWIP visibility, stronger 

approval governance and a structured transition from project expenditure to final fixed-asset capitalization.
