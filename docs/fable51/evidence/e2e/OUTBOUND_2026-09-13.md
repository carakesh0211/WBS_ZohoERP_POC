## What it took (the record, not smoothed over)

1. The launcher stripped `CAPEX_ERP_OUTBOUND_WRITES` in every stage
   (`tools/appsail/uat_main.py`), so four console edits and recycles could
   not open the gate. Fixed at `96c72e2`: Stage B honours exactly `1`.
2. The first CREATE-scoped refresh token was issued from a grant WITHOUT
   CREATE; the app's "granted" list was the configured string, so it could
   not tell. Fixed at `c977c21`: the transport records the scope Zoho
   reports on each mint and token health names the gap.
3. While diagnosing (1), a Catalyst read tool returned the AppSail
   configuration with its values; the refresh token (since replaced), the
   client secret and the database password passed through the session.
   Rotation of the latter two is recommended after the test.
