1. About the application

The CAPEX & WBS Control Hub is the capital-expenditure control system for projects structured as a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS). It holds the approved budget for every project, WBS element and budget head; checks every purchase request against the money actually available; runs maker-checker approvals; commits purchase orders; reads receipts and vendor bills back from Zoho ERP; reconciles ordered, received and billed values; and carries the project to capitalisation with a complete, tamper-evident audit trail.

What it controls

  • Budget per control cell (project → WBS element → budget head), with revisions, transfers and version comparison.
  • Exposure = open PO commitment + actual CWIP (billed) + purchase-request reserves + internal material allocation + internal consumption. Available budget is budget minus exposure. Nothing is approved past what is available without a recorded exception.
  • Maker-checker on every approval: the person who raised or edited an object may not approve it. An Administrator may override this deliberately, with a reason, and the override is recorded and notified.
  • Money is always integer paise internally and shown in Indian format (₹, lakh, crore). Foreign-currency orders keep their currency and rate.
  • Audit: every change is an append-only, hash-chained entry.

2. The UAT environment and what the banner means

The yellow banner at the top of every page reads UAT — SYNTHETIC DATA — ZOHO ERP DEMO TENANT 60074128927 — WRITES ENABLED. It means:

  • Synthetic data: projects, budgets, vendors and documents are test data. Nothing here is a real transaction.
  • Zoho ERP demo tenant: the connector is bound to one demo organisation only. Purchase orders approved here can be emitted to that tenant; receipts and bills from it are read back.
  • Writes enabled: outbound purchase-order creation to the demo tenant is switched on under permanent boundaries (one organisation, purchase orders only, every order must be approved here first, identical retries never create duplicates).
What testers must not do: do not treat any figure as real; do not expect e-mail to arrive (mail is recorded, not sent, until the owner verifies a sender); do not share your test password; report anything surprising rather than working around it.

4. Signing in, forgotten passwords, changing a password

Sign in with a WBS account

Open the address, enter your user id and password, and press Sign in. Repeated failures are throttled; wait a few minutes and try again.

Forgot password

  1. Click Forgot password?, enter your user id, press Send reset link.
  2. A reset link valid for 15 minutes, usable once, is generated and queued as a notification.
  3. On UAT, mail is recorded rather than sent. Ask the Administrator, who can release the link to you from the notification outbox when the owner has enabled that (section 8). Once a real sender is verified, the link arrives by e-mail.
  4. Open the link, choose a new password, confirm. Every other session you had is signed out.

Password policy: at least 12 characters with upper case, lower case and a digit; not your user id; not any of your last five passwords; not the seeded starter password.

Change password (signed in)

Use Change password in the top bar. You enter the current password and the new one twice. Your current session continues; every other session is signed out.

  • Left rail: screens are grouped (Work, Project Control, Procurement & Actuals, Closure, Integration, Governance, Analytics, Integration Mapping). Every group is a collapsible button; Expand all / Collapse all act on every group. Your choice is remembered per browser tab. The group holding the screen you are on is always opened.
  • Top bar: entity and plant filters (they scope the registers and the exports), the Compact density toggle, badges for Approvals and Alerts waiting for you, then your name and role, Notifications (your preferences and history), Change password, Sign out.
  • Breadcrumb and page actions: under the top bar. Create buttons and export controls sit on the right of the page title.
  • Narrow screens: below 900 px the rail becomes a drawer opened with the ☰ button.
  • Deep links: every screen has an address (#prs, #imrs, #approval-inbox…). Back and Forward work as expected.

7. How to operate: the core procedures

7.1 Raise and approve a purchase request

  1. Requestor / BudgetController: Purchase Requests → Create purchase request. Choose project, WBS element, budget head, describe the need and enter the value. Save (Draft), then Submit.
  2. On submission the system checks the control cell. Within budget → status Submitted, routed to a ProcurementApprover. Over budget → status Exception Pending, routed to a FinanceApprover; the shortfall is shown and the reason is mandatory.
  3. Approver: My Approvals or the register → Approve (or reject with a reason). You cannot approve a request you raised or edited: the server refuses with SELF_APPROVAL. An Administrator may override this deliberately (section 8).
  4. Approved requests reserve budget (PR reserve) until converted or released.

7.2 Convert to a purchase order and emit to Zoho ERP

  1. Commitments (PO) → Raise purchase order from an approved request; choose vendor and currency (INR by default; a foreign currency needs a rate from the rate book, otherwise the order is refused as FX_RATE_UNAVAILABLE).
  2. The order commits budget (open commitment) and releases the request's reserve.
  3. Emit sends the order to the Zoho ERP demo tenant once; the outbox row keeps the external id, and an identical retry creates nothing new. Every emitted order carries the CAPEX reference, WBS code and budget head as custom fields.
  4. Amend, cancel and close are ProcurementApprover actions on the order row.

7.3 Receipts, bills and reconciliation

Receipts and vendor bills are read from Zoho ERP by the sweeps (Zoho ERP Connector → sweeps, or the scheduled run). A receipt moves value to received-not-billed; a bill posts actual CWIP and reduces open commitment. Commitment Reconciliation shows ordered, received, billed and open per line and raises coded exceptions (for example an unattributed receipt line, a foreign-currency bill without a rate basis, an order whose dimensions do not match).

7.4 Internal material requests (material from stores)

  1. On a purchase-request line, a fulfilment decision (BudgetController or ProcurementApprover) chooses External purchase, Internal transfer, or Split with an internal quantity. Internal material needs a valuation: from the inventory provider when mapped, otherwise a manual unit rate with a written note.
  2. The decision raises the internal material request (REQUESTED). Approve (ProcurementApprover; maker-checker applies) → APPROVED.
  3. Allocate moves the money from the request's reserve to internal allocation; Issue moves it to internal consumption (CWIP); Return gives it back; Consume confirms use; Transfer moves stock between stores without money; Cancel releases everything while still reserved.
  4. Only the external portion of a split line converts to a purchase order; a fully internal line cannot be converted.

7.5 Budget revisions

Budget Revisions → Request revision (Supplement or Transfer, with reason). A FinanceApprover approves; the original budget stays visible and the revision is a separate line. Transfers need both cells in your scope.

7.6 Capitalisation

Capitalisation → allocate CWIP to assets (BudgetController or FinanceApprover) → Approve capitalisation (CapitalisationApprover). A project with open commitment is blocked with CAPITALISATION_BLOCKED until the commitment is closed.

8. Administrator tasks

8.1 The deliberate self-approval override

When an Administrator approves an object it raised or edited, the server first refuses with SELF_APPROVAL. The dialog then shows an unmissable warning and asks for a reason of at least 10 characters. Confirming re-sends the approval with the reason. The result:

  • the approval is applied;
  • an audit entry ADMIN_SELF_APPROVAL_OVERRIDE records who, when, the object and the reason;
  • every other Administrator is notified.

The override cannot be delegated and is never offered for period reopening. Use it only when no independent approver exists.

8.2 Releasing a password-reset link on UAT

While mail is recorded rather than sent, the only copy of a reset link is in the notification outbox and its body is hidden even from Administrators. If the platform owner has enabled the UAT reveal switch, open Governance → Notifications, find the PASSWORD_RESET_REQUESTED row for the user, open it and pass the link to the person. Every reveal is recorded in the audit trail as NOTIFICATION_BODY_REVEALED against your name. The switch stops working the moment real mail is enabled.

8.3 Dispatching the outbox

Governance → Notifications → Dispatch due notifications sends everything queued or due for retry. Nothing is sent inside a business transaction; dispatch is separate and safe to repeat (a second dispatch claims nothing). When the owner sets the dispatch interval, the ticker does this every minute.

8.4 Linking a Zoho identity to a user

Once single sign-on is configured, a person's Zoho identity must be linked to their existing WBS user before "Continue with Zoho" works for them; an unlinked sign-in is refused and audited. Linking is an Administrator API action (POST /api/admin/users/{user_id}/identities with the Zoho subject). No user is ever created by a Zoho sign-in, and no role ever comes from Zoho.

8.5 Delegations

Delegation Management lets an approver delegate an authority they hold, for a period, to someone who is independent of the object. A delegate who contributed to the object is refused exactly as the maker would be.

9. Exports (CSV and Excel)

Every register, dashboard and closure list carries an export control in the page actions, named after its dataset (for example Purchase requests and Purchase request lines on the same screen). Press Export CSV or Export XLSX. The export runs as a job that your browser drives; keep the page open until Download appears, then click it. The entity and plant filters in the top bar are applied to the export.

  • The Excel workbook has four sheets: Summary, Data, Applied Filters, Metadata. Money cells are numbers in Indian format with a SUM total; dates are real dates in UTC; text is text.
  • Excel is capped at 20,000 rows; a larger export is refused honestly. Use CSV or narrow the filters.
  • An export job expires after a day; run it again if the download has gone.

10. Notifications

Notifications in the top bar opens your preferences and your own delivery history. You can switch off informational events; security notices (password reset, password changed, identity linked, Administrator override) cannot be switched off. Events wired today: PR submitted, PR approved, IMR approved, Administrator self-approval override, export completed, exception raised, password reset requested, password changed, identity linked.

On UAT every notification is recorded, not sent. Delivery rows show RECORDED. E-mail leaves the platform only after the owner verifies a sender domain and switches the adapter (section 14).

11. Governance: audit trail and controls

Governance → Audit Trail lists every entry, filterable by stream, object type and id, action and actor, with a table and a timeline view. Verify checks the hash chain of a stream. The chain is intact; the daily anchor that signs each day's chain (a scheduled Catalyst function) is not yet running on UAT, so verification reports NEVER_ANCHORED. That is a scheduling gap, not tampering.

Other controls testers will meet: budget-head isolation (money never moves between heads without a revision), ordered ancestor locking (concurrent approvals on the same cells cannot double-spend), idempotency keys on every write, and refusals with a code and a sentence rather than a silent failure.

13. Frequently asked questions

I cannot sign in. What should I check?
User id exactly as issued (they are upper case, like U-REQ), the correct password, and whether several failures have throttled you: wait a few minutes. If you still cannot sign in, use Forgot password or ask the Administrator.
Why is there no "Continue with Zoho" button?
The owner has not yet configured the Zoho single sign-on client. The button appears automatically once all its settings exist. WBS sign-in always works.
I asked for a reset link and no e-mail came.
On UAT mail is recorded, not sent. The Administrator can release the link from the notification outbox if the owner has enabled that; otherwise ask the Administrator to set a temporary password through the coordinator.
A screen I need is missing from the menu.
Your role does not hold its permission. The menu only offers what the server will allow; typing the address is corrected back to Home.
My request went to "Exception Pending" instead of "Submitted".
The control cell has less available budget than the value requested. It is routed to a FinanceApprover with the shortfall shown; a reason is required. Alternatively raise a budget revision first.
"SELF_APPROVAL" when I press Approve.
You raised or edited that object. Ask an independent approver. An Administrator may override with a written reason; the override is recorded and notified.
The Approve button is not shown to me on a row.
Approval needs the specific permission for that stage (ProcurementApprover for within-budget, FinanceApprover for exceptions and revisions, CapitalisationApprover for capitalisation), and the object must be in a decidable state.
My export stays at "queued".
Your browser drives the export; keep the page open. If it was closed, open the screen again and press Export again.
Excel export refused: "too large".
The workbook is capped at 20,000 rows. Use CSV, or narrow the entity/plant filters.
Why does the PO screen show Amend/Cancel/Close for some users and not others?
Those are ProcurementApprover (and Administrator) actions; other roles see "not permitted for your role".
Internal material request refused with INTERNAL_MAPPING_MISSING or VALUATION_NOTE_REQUIRED.
The cell has no budget-owning mapping, or a manual valuation was entered without the note that explains its source. Fix the mapping or add the note; the exception is retracted when resolved.
"IMR_ALREADY_LIVE".
A line can carry one live internal request at a time. Cancel or complete the existing one first.
The audit verify says NEVER_ANCHORED. Was the log tampered with?
No. The chain is intact; the daily anchoring schedule is not yet running on UAT. Whole-stream deletion cannot be detected until it is.
Control totals show 503 CONTROL_TOTALS_UNAVAILABLE.
By design: the tenant provides no endpoint for its side of the comparison. Reconciliation still balances line by line.
Will the demo Zoho tenant receive real orders from my testing?
Only an approved order that you explicitly emit is written, to the demo tenant only, once. Nothing else is written to Zoho.
A notification I expected is missing from my history.
Notifications go to users who have an e-mail address on their PostgreSQL profile and hold the receiving role in the object's project scope. Check your preferences, and ask the Administrator to look in the outbox.
Where are figures in "L" and "Cr"?
Lakh (₹1,00,000) and crore (₹1,00,00,000), Indian grouping. Detail tables show full rupees; internally everything is paise.
The page shows "This screen could not be loaded".
A module failed to load, usually a network hiccup. Reload the page; if it persists, report it with the time and screen name.
